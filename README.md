# Hi, I'm Madhav.

I'm a software engineer interested in building reliable systems and understanding the ideas that make complex systems work.

My engineering experience spans **backend and platform development, enterprise data integrations, cloud infrastructure, and applied AI**. I particularly enjoy problems where correctness, performance, and architecture intersect.

Outside engineering, I'm drawn to mathematics, theoretical computer science, philosophy, and questions about intelligence, abstraction, and information.

## Engineering

Some of the areas I've worked on include:

- **Enterprise platforms:** Building and operating multi-tenant SaaS infrastructure across cloud environments.
- **Data integration:** Developing connectors, APIs, and synchronization workflows connecting enterprise systems.
- **Performance and reliability:** Investigating database bottlenecks, improving data-processing throughput, and designing recoverable background workflows.
- **Applied AI:** Building and evaluating AI-powered applications and exploring agent-assisted software engineering.

I care about understanding *why* systems behave the way they do—not just making them work under ideal conditions.

## Research

My research interests extend beyond any single field, particularly toward the mathematical and computational foundations of intelligence and information.

## Questions I'm Exploring

A few questions I find especially interesting:

- How do useful abstractions emerge from complex problems?
- What makes one representation of information more powerful than another?

I hope to explore some of these questions through independent projects, experiments, and writing.
